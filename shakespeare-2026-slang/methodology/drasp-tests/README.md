## Major findings retained

- Modern slang is **not inherently incompatible** with Shakespearean blank verse; incompatibility may arise from placement, syntax, stress clustering, or performance register.
- A line can be mathematically scannable yet theatrically fail because a fixed idiom pulls delivery into conversational prose.
- Rhyme should be tested and, where necessary, compensated rather than silently dropped or mechanically forced.
- Transactional prose can absorb high-density digital vernacular with very low structural friction when the source and slang share communicative goals.
- Philosophical and prophetic prose can be structurally effortless yet semantically narrowed by platform-specific or meme-bound language.
- Beat-level dramatic gravity is more precise than classifying vocabulary by comedy versus tragedy alone.
