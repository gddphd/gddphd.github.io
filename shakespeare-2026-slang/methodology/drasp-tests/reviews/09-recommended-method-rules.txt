DRASP Trigger and Evidentiary Baseline
Following the mandatory full escalation sequence of DRASP Policy A, synthesizing the
structural limitations identified across blank verse, rhyming couplets, and philosophical prose
requires an actionable methodological framework. To prevent future translational failures—such
as metrical collapse, syntactic strain, or tonal deflation—the cumulative findings of the corpus
must be codified into definitive rules.
Actionable Translation Guidelines for the Romeo Protocol
● Rule 1: Genre-Specific Lexical Gating (The Tonal Boundary)
○ Directive: Restrict high-colloquialism and meme-centric slang strictly to comedies,
transactional prose, and character-driven scenes (e.g., Midsummer's mechanicals).
○ Rationale: As demonstrated by the King Lear storm scene, applying internet
vernacular to high tragedy or philosophical prose introduces cynical detachment
and catastrophic tonal deflation.
● Rule 2: Mandatory Digital Accommodations in Tragedies
○ Directive: Replace colloquial slang with conceptual, system-level digital
accommodations (such as Lear's "hard reset" or Midsummer's "admin rights") when
adapting high-stakes dramatic works.
○ Rationale: System-level terminology preserves the severity, power structures, and
cosmic gravity of tragedies without relying on lightweight internet humor.
● Rule 3: Total Syntactic Rebuilding for Verse Integration
○ Directive: Never force spondaic or polysyllabic slang (e.g., "speedrun," "hard block")
into a rigid Elizabethan iambic footprint. The surrounding connective syntax must be
entirely overhauled to accommodate the modern phonetic weight.
○ Rationale: Direct word substitution creates awkward consonant clusters and
metrical collapse; smooth integration requires restructuring the line's rhythmic flow
around the slang's natural stress.
● Rule 4: Systematic Abandonment of End-Rhyme
○ Directive: Deliberately discard strict phonetic rhyme schemes when translating
complex rhymed verse (such as prophecies or sonnets) into contemporary lexicons.
○ Rationale: Enforcing an end-rhyme with inflexible modern idioms forces absurd
syntactic inversions and infantilized phrasing; sacrificing the rhyme preserves the
pragmatic and satirical accuracy of the text.
● Rule 5: Prioritization of Transactional Prose
○ Directive: Leverage prose sections as the primary vehicle for high-density slang
integration.
○ Rationale: Because prose lacks a fixed syllabic grid, its structural elasticity naturally
absorbs conversational digital idioms without the friction experienced in verse.
Synthesis Gate and Audit Conclusion
The synthesis gate confirms that these guidelines resolve the systemic vulnerabilities identified
during corpus evaluation. By enforcing genre bifurcation, syntactic rebuilding, and the strategic
surrender of rhyme, future iterations of the Romeo Protocol can maintain both structural rigor
and emotional fidelity.
