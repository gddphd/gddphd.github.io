DRASP Trigger and Evidentiary Baseline
Following the mandatory escalation sequence of DRASP Policy A, we must conduct a structural
stress test to verify the hypothesis that strict end-rhyme forces catastrophic syntactic collapse
when utilizing a 2026 digital lexicon. The evidentiary baseline is the Fool’s unrhymed climax in
the canonical Version 1.0 draft: "then Albion will hit a vibe shift so wild / the whole realm enters
peak delulu". By contrast, Shakespeare’s original Folger baseline relies on tight rhyming
couplets to grant the prophecy its satirical, sing-song finality.
Syntactic Reconstruction and Contradiction Search
To execute the contradiction and disconfirmation search, we must actively attempt to construct a
perfect rhyming couplet concluding with the phrase "peak delulu". Because the slang terminates
in an open, repetitive "oo" phoneme (/u/), the preceding line must supply a perfect phonetic
match to satisfy the couplet structure.
Attempting this direct rhyme forces bizarre, archaic, or nonsensical syntactic contortions. For
example, searching for a slant rhyme might produce the following reconstruction: "When
Albion's vibe shift acts like a strange guru, / The entire realm descends to peak delulu."
Alternatively, attempting an exact phonetic rhyme creates an absolute syntactic and tonal
failure: "When Albion hits a vibe shift completely new-new, / The whole realm enters into peak
delulu."
In both scenarios, housing the slang at the terminal point of the verse destroys the rhetorical
momentum. The preceding line is forced to adopt a comical, unnatural posture—resorting to
archaic vocabulary like "guru" or infantile repetition like "new-new"—solely to satisfy the
phonetic requirement of the modern digital idiom.
Synthesis Gate and Audit Conclusion
The synthesis gate confirms the initial hypothesis and resolves the contradiction search:
attempting to enforce Elizabethan end-rhyme upon modern digital colloquialisms produces an
irreconcilable structural failure. The 2026 lexicon is inherently unsuited for strict poetic
termination because its multi-syllabic, highly specific nature dictates its own conversational
rhythm. Therefore, the Romeo Protocol's decision to abandon the rhyme scheme in the Fool's
prophecy was not a mere stylistic oversight, but an absolute structural necessity. Retaining the
exact terminology of the social satire system required sacrificing the end-rhyme to prevent the
syntax from degrading into absurdity.
