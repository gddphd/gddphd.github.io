# AI Assistance Disclosure

This project was developed through iterative human-directed work with AI systems.

## ChatGPT role

ChatGPT was used under the project owner's direction to assist with:

- drafting and revising contemporary Shakespeare adaptations;
- lexical classification and annotation;
- prosodic and rhetorical analysis;
- corpus counting and revision ledgers;
- development and formalization of the Romeo Protocol;
- synthesis of external methodological reviews;
- document generation and repository organization.

The project owner selected scenes, directed editorial goals, required version preservation, supplied the primary slang glossary and external reviews, and controlled whether protocol drafts were adopted.

## Gemini role

External analyses supplied by the project owner and attributed to Gemini were used as methodological review material. Their useful findings include critiques of prosodic friction, tonal deflation, fixed multi-word idioms, rhyme conflicts, and differences among prose modalities.

Gemini's findings are credited in Romeo Protocol Revisions B-D. They were not incorporated automatically: later protocol revisions distinguish Accepted, Qualified, and Rejected/Unproven claims.

## Human editorial responsibility

AI-generated material should be treated as an editorial/research product requiring human review. The protocol itself includes evidence gates, ambiguity controls, performance tests, revision preservation, and adverse-event accounting precisely because generated translations and analyses can contain errors or overgeneralizations.

## Shakespeare source status

The Shakespearean source text underlying these experiments is public-domain material in the United States. AI participation in adaptation or analysis does not alter the need to distinguish the public-domain source from newly created editorial and adaptation material.

## Versioning

AI-assisted revisions do not silently replace earlier artifacts. Historical versions remain in the repository so changes in methodology and translation judgment can be inspected.
