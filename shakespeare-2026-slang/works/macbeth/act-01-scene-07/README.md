# Macbeth - Act I, Scene vii

## 2026 GENEX / Alpha Slang Reconstruction

**Current repository status:** Working Draft v0.2 - high-density prosodic pass.

This scene extends the Shakespeare 2026 Slang corpus into *Macbeth* and is designed as an M4 stress test for dense contemporary slang inside tragic blank verse. It preserves Macbeth's moral hesitation, Lady Macbeth's coercive escalation, the infanticide image, the murder logistics, and the appearance-versus-intent structure while testing whether recurring digital-era lexical systems can remain theatrically and prosodically viable.
