# Macbeth - Act I, Scene vii
## 2026 GENEX / Alpha Slang Reconstruction
### Working Draft v0.2 - High-Density Prosodic Pass

**Status:** Working draft. This package supersedes the earlier restrained Macbeth chat pass as the current Macbeth candidate, but it is not yet designated canonical.

**Protocol status:** The established Romeo Protocol baseline remains the controlling floor. This draft is additionally evaluated using the still-draft v2.0 Revision D controls for prosody, dramatic gravity, lexical rigidity, pragmatic alignment, tonal deflation, and universality.

## 1. Revision Transition Note

This document packages the second Macbeth I.vii reconstruction. The earlier pass deliberately used a moderate M2-M3 modernization density. The present v0.2 pass responds to the project owner's direction to increase substitutions substantially while retaining Shakespearean meter as far as contemporary pronunciation permits.

The revision therefore moves toward **M4 high-density reconstruction**. It does not equate density with quality: the infant-killing speech remains comparatively restrained because meme-heavy substitution there would reduce horror, coercive force, and tragic gravity.

## 2. Editorial Objectives

- Preserve Macbeth's movement from consequence analysis to refusal.
- Preserve Lady Macbeth's movement from ridicule to coercion to operational planning.
- Increase contemporary slang density through recurring semantic systems rather than isolated jokes.
- Rebuild connective syntax around the natural stress of modern terms.
- Preserve a five-stress Shakespearean movement where feasible without forcing artificial pronunciation.
- Distinguish PDF-attested slang, slang-derived variants, supplemental usage, digital-era accommodations, and project-created wording.
- Preserve the earlier Macbeth pass in revision history rather than silently replacing it.

## 4. Canonical Candidate Translation

*Hautboys. Torches. Servants cross with dishes. Macbeth enters.*

**MACBETH**

If done were done, I'd **speedrun** to the end.
If one clean hit could **hard-delete** fallout,
And trap the consequence, then cash the win,
So this one move became the whole **main quest**,
I'd risk what follows and just take the play.

But judgment still lives here; we teach men blood,
And what we teach comes back with **receipts**.
Fair justice keeps the screenshots of the crime,
Then **drops the proof** and serves our cup to us.

He's here in double trust: my king and kin,
Two giant **red flags** on this murder plan.
And I'm his host; I ought to **hard-block** knives,
Not be the **opp** who lets the blade get in.

Besides - **no cap** - this Duncan's ruled with grace;
His record's clean, his virtues speak too loud.
They'll **fact-check** every story after death,
And prove the damned evil of the kill.

His pity, like a newborn in the storm,
Will ride the winds and make the horror **viral**,
Till every eye gets flooded by the grief
And tears outpace the rain across the land.

I've got no drive to push this murder on,
Except ambition, **highkey locked too hard**,
That vaults the build, gets **cooked**, and eats the fall.

*Enter Lady Macbeth.*

How now? What news?

**LADY MACBETH**

He's nearly done.
Why did you dip and leave the feast so soon?

**MACBETH**

Has he asked for me?

**LADY MACBETH**

You know he has.

**MACBETH**

We will not **cook this plan**. I'm done with it.
He's honored me; my **clout** is peaking now,
And everybody's **hard-launching** my praise.
I should not **hard-delete** that fresh respect
Or **speedrun** straight from glory into shame.

**LADY MACBETH**

Was all that hope just **cap** when you first wore it?
Was courage drunk, then sleeping off the **vibe**,
And now wakes pale and scared of its own dream?

From now on that's the **vibe check** on your love.

Are you afraid to **lock in** what you want?
You want the crown - the ultimate **hard flex** -
Yet call yourself a coward in your heart.

You call the throne your one and only **main quest**,
Then let "I dare not" **hard-block** "I would."

That **math ain't mathing**. Is your courage real?
You want the prize but will not get hands wet.

**MACBETH**

Enough. I dare do all that fits a man.
Who goes beyond is not a man at all.

**LADY MACBETH**

Then what damn beast made you **hard-launch** this
To me, when first you pitched the murder plan?

When you would do it, then you were a man;
To be still more, you'd **level up** the deed.

No time, no place were ready when you spoke,
Yet you would make them both. And now they're here -
The timing and the place have **locked in** clean -
And this whole **clutch** alignment cooks your nerve.

You're staring at your own successful setup
And somehow getting **cooked** by what you wanted.

I've given suck. I know how tenderly
One loves the infant feeding at the breast.

Yet while it smiled and trusted in my face,
I would have torn my nipple from its gums
And dashed its brains out, had I sworn to do it
As you have sworn that you would carry this.

**MACBETH**

If we should fail -

**LADY MACBETH**

We fail?

Then **lock in** courage till it cannot shake,
And we won't fail. **No cap.** When Duncan sleeps -
And this hard journey will have cooked him down -
His two attendants I will drown with wine.

I'll pour till memory takes a **hard reset**,
And reason simply **logs off** from the brain.
Their guarded sense will turn to smoke and fog;
Their bodies lie like death beneath the drink.

When wine and sleep have got them fully **cooked**,
What can't we do to Duncan while he lies?
What can't we pin upon those wasted guards?

We use their blades. We smear them with his blood.
We manufacture all the bloody **receipts**
And make the evidence look **main verified**.

They wake to one enormous **reality check**:
The king is dead, their knives are red with blood,
And every single sign points back to them.

**MACBETH**

Bring forth men-children only; that bold nerve
Should build no timid thing. Your fearless **build**
Could spawn no daughters made of softer stuff.

But tell me - will they buy this **lore** we drop,
When Duncan's blood is painted on the guards,
His daggers planted where they slept it off?

Will all receive that story as the truth?

**LADY MACBETH**

Who dares call **cap** when grief goes through the roof?
We'll cry so loud the whole house knows the **vibe**.
We'll act the outrage, sell the shock as real,
And drown all other theories in the noise.

Who's asking us for **receipts** when we grieve?
Who'll **fact-check** royal sorrow in that hour?

**MACBETH**

**Say less.**

I'm settled. Every nerve is **locked in** now.
I bend each power of body to this act.

Come. We must **curate** what the world will see.
Our faces need to pass the **vibe check** clean.

Keep **mask on**; let the friendly face conceal
The dark intent we keep **off-feed** within.
Let every feature **soft-launch** innocence
Until the bloody **main quest** has gone live.

*They exit.*

## 7. Prosodic Reconstruction Audit

The draft targets **five principal stresses** rather than mechanical ten-syllable regularity. Natural contemporary pronunciation is not to be distorted merely to preserve inherited scansion. Where slang creates a heavy stress cluster, syntax should be rebuilt or the term moved to a metrically licensed position.

| Line | Prosodic strategy | Status | Audit note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| If done were done, I'd speedrun to the end. | Five-stress target; opening stress concentration | Near-pass | The repeated DONE stresses suit Macbeth's obsessive conditional logic; speedrun is kept away from another heavy compound. |
| And what we teach comes back with receipts. | Rising five-beat tendency | Pass / flexible | Receipts lands late where its natural stress can carry the close. |
| And I'm his host; I ought to hard-block knives, | Caesural reset before heavy compound | Near-pass | Host/ought/hard/block/knives produce a forceful stress chain; actor pacing is important. |
| From now on that's the vibe check on your love. | Five principal stresses | Pass / flexible | The two-syllable compound sits in a stable medial position. |
| Are you afraid to lock in what you want? | Strong rising movement | Pass | Lock in cooperates unusually well with the rhetorical question. |
| That math ain't mathing. Is your courage real? | Caesura-driven two-part line | Pass / flexible | The phrase is allowed to speak naturally; the caesura resets the second half. |
| Then lock in courage till it cannot shake, | Initial inversion permitted | Pass / flexible | Imperative opening justifies a heavy first beat. |
| I'll pour till memory takes a hard reset, | Five-stress target with heavy terminal phrase | Near-pass | Hard reset is conceptually strong but requires actor stress testing. |
| We manufacture all the bloody receipts | Expanded / feminine tendency | Near-pass | Longer syntax is tolerated because evidence manufacture is rhetorically cumulative. |
| I'm settled. Every nerve is locked in now. | Caesural reset + five-beat tendency | Pass / flexible | Strong close on now; natural speech remains intact. |
| Let every feature soft-launch innocence | Five-stress target; polysyllabic ending | Stress-test | Soft-launch plus innocence is intentionally flagged for rehearsal and possible rebuild. |

### Prosodic risk queue

The following phrases require actor read-aloud testing before canonical adoption:

- **hard-launching my praise**
- **highkey locked too hard**
- **main verified**
- **hard reset**
- **soft-launch innocence**
- **off-feed within**

If any of these force unnatural stress, swallowed syllables, or prose-like delivery, the complete line should be rebuilt rather than merely trimming syllables.

## 8. Dramatic-Fidelity Audit

**Macbeth's moral calculus:** Preserved. The adaptation retains consequence, reciprocal justice, kinship/subject duty, host obligation, Duncan's virtues, pity, and vaulting ambition.

**Lady Macbeth's coercion:** Preserved. Her strategy still attacks the gap between Macbeth's desire and willingness to act, questions his masculinity, invokes her own hypothetical infanticide as proof of resolve, and then supplies the operational murder plan.

**Infant speech protection:** Deliberately low slang density. This is a G4-G5 gravity pocket inside an otherwise M4 adaptation. The protocol treats the relative absence of slang here as a fidelity decision, not a missed opportunity.

**Evidence fabrication:** Strengthened through the recurring receipts / fact-check / verified / reality-check system, which tracks the source scene's move from Macbeth's fear of judgment to the couple's plan to manufacture blame.

**Public-face motif:** Strengthened through clout / hard-launch / curate / vibe-check / mask-on / soft-launch language, while preserving the original contrast between outward appearance and concealed intent.
