# Romeo and Juliet

## Corpus role

*Romeo and Juliet*, Act II, Scene II is the originating methodological reference for the Shakespeare-in-2026-Slang corpus. The annotated prosodic reconstruction established the working floor later called the **Romeo Protocol**.

- [`Act II, Scene II — canonical annotated DOCX`](act-02-scene-02/Shakespeare_2026_Slang_Annotated_Romeo_and_Juliet_II_ii.docx)
- [`Act II, Scene II — exact-text archive`](act-02-scene-02/archive/README.md)

The exact-text archive is split into three ordered files for repository transport. Its README records the reconstruction command and SHA-256 checksum.

## Methodological contribution

The scene established the project requirements for:

- preserving dramatic sequence and character differentiation;
- maintaining a Shakespearean prosodic orientation where feasible;
- aggressive lexical reconstruction rather than isolated word swapping;
- PDF-first slang sourcing with separately identified supplemental contemporary evidence;
- bold markup for slang and accommodation wording;
- annotation of source function, rationale, equivalence strength, evidence status, and prosodic accommodation;
- coherent recurring lexical systems;
- scene-level and cumulative lexical accounting.
