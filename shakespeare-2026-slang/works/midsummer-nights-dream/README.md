# A Midsummer Night's Dream

## Corpus role

*A Midsummer Night's Dream*, Act I, Scenes I-II became the first major cross-play test of the Romeo Protocol. It is especially important for character-specific slang density, comic prose, relationship/power vocabulary, and the transition from sparse modernization to a full Romeo-protocol reconstruction.
