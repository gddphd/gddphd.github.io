The method intentionally preserves the logical sequence of Shakespeare's argument even where the imagery changes. "New meta" is a gaming-derived metaphor for the emergent strategy of patience. "Ghost from sight" was already established in the Romeo project as a modern disappearance analogue.

### Helena system

Helena receives a lexical density closer to Romeo: **be so for real**, **ate**, **aura**, **glow up**, **rizz**, **drag**, **clutch**, **down horrendous**, **skill issue**, **ghost**, **hellscape**, **lore dump**, **girl therapy**, **era**, **close friends only**, **mid**, **math ain't mathing**, **peak delulu**, **hard-deleted**, **tea time**, **down bad**, and **we move**.

This system translates:
- envy of Hermia's perceived desirability;
- asymmetry in romantic pursuit;
- Helena's friendship with Hermia;
- the escape-plan disclosure;
- Helena's philosophical analysis of love;
- her knowingly self-defeating decision to tell Demetrius.

The line:

Love looks not with the eyes but with the mind;
the **math ain't mathing**, therefore Cupid's blind.

is a representative Romeo-protocol collision: current slang takes over the argumentative function of the original line rather than merely decorating it.

## Canonical Annotation and Equivalence Notes - Act I, Scene 2

Bottom's role now carries the highest slang density because Shakespeare already characterizes him through interruption, theatrical overconfidence, self-promotion, and eagerness to play every role.

**Locked in**, **lore dump**, **peak**, **no cap**, **slaps hard**, **cook and eat**, **lowkey**, **mid**, **hard-carry**, **cook**, **mog**, and **hits different** create Bottom's self-evaluating performance vocabulary.

The "cook" family is particularly productive: the glossary supports cook as brilliant creation/delivery and cook-and-eat as creating and performing excellently. Equivalence strength: strong character/pragmatic.

**Hard-carry** means one player single-handedly wins. Because Bottom repeatedly tries to take multiple roles, this is a strong metaphorical analogue.

**Let me cook**, **clown behavior**, **Pack it up**, **Say less**, **Bet**, **Big yikes**, and **skill issue** translate the tug-of-war between Bottom's interruptions and Quince's attempts to control rehearsal.

**Soft-launch** is used comically for Bottom's proposed gentle lion roar: instead of revealing a relationship, he would reveal terror gradually. This is a deliberately loose but internally coherent extension of an established project term.

**Main character**, **GOAT**, **drip**, and **drip check** translate Bottom's self-elevation and beard/costume obsession. Equivalence strength: strong comic-functional.

**Tea time** maps Quince's fear that rehearsing in town would reveal the group's plans. **Feral**, **yap**, **slay**, and **leave no crumbs** modernize the rehearsal's secrecy and Bottom's prediction of flawless performance.

**We ride at moonrise** is a creative derived variant of the attested "we ride at dawn." It preserves the rallying-cry function while changing the time to match the mechanicals' actual moonlit rehearsal. It is tracked separately from exact attested slang in the conservative ledger.
