## Methodological significance

The scene demonstrated that lexical density can legitimately vary inside one scene: Stephano and Trinculo sustain dense comic slang, while Caliban's famous lyrical speech is deliberately less saturated. This became an important precursor to the later gravity, prose-modality, and universality controls.
