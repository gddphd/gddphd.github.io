2. CONTROLLING EDITORIAL PROTOCOL - ROMEO PROTOCOL
======================================================================

All Shakespeare-to-2026-slang translations in this project must, at minimum, match the Romeo and Juliet protocol.

The controlling requirements are:

1. Preserve Shakespeare's dramatic sequence, character relationships, argumentative structure, and recognizable rhetorical architecture.
2. Maintain an iambic-pentameter / Shakespearean prosodic orientation where feasible; do not falsely claim mechanical ten-syllable regularity where the line does not sustain it.
3. Use aggressive contemporary lexical reconstruction rather than sparse slang insertion.
4. Use the user-supplied slang PDF as the primary slang lexicon.
5. Permit separately corroborated contemporary slang when the PDF does not contain a needed term, but label that evidentiary status clearly.
6. Allow surrounding syntax to be rewritten when necessary so slang feels structurally integrated rather than pasted into archaic prose.
7. Bold slang and wording materially rewritten to accommodate the contemporary lexical layer.
8. Annotate each major lexical intervention by:
   - Shakespearean source/function,
   - 2026 replacement,
   - semantic rationale,
   - equivalence strength,
   - source status,
   - metrical/prosodic accommodation where relevant.
9. Maintain coherent recurring lexical systems across scenes and works.
10. Maintain scene-level and cumulative project-wide counts.
11. Preserve revision history and superseded versions rather than silently erasing them.
12. Distinguish slang, slang-derived variants, supplemental contemporary slang, and non-slang digital-era accommodation.

This protocol is a floor, not a ceiling.

B. PRIMARY SLANG LEXICON

User-supplied PDF:
"Social Media Slang: Emoji and Acronym Meanings Teens are Using."

Primary glossary evidence used in this pass includes:
- Cap: lie or exaggeration.
- No cap: truthful; not lying.
- Voice filter: altered audio effect overlay.
- Clown behavior: foolish conduct drawing mockery.
- Pack it up: stop; it is over.
- Bet: agreement or challenge accepted.
- Lore dump: large disclosure of backstory.
- Main quest: primary life goal/task metaphor.
- Side quest: secondary task or distraction.
- Gaslight: manipulate someone's sense of reality.
- Delulu: delusional optimism or fantasy.
- Out of pocket: inappropriate or shocking behavior.
- Nerf her: make a character weaker.
- Main character syndrome: excessive self-centered narrative.
- W: win / success achieved.
- Yap: excessive talking.
- Receipts: proof supporting claims.
- This ain't it: disapproval / wrong approach.
- Unsauced: uncredited or lacking source.
- Jump scare: sudden unpleasant surprise.
- Hits different: unusually impactful or resonant.
- Soft launch: subtly reveal something new.
- It slaps: sounds great / very good.

C. DERIVED / PRODUCTIVE FORMS

The following forms are derived from glossary-attested vocabulary:
- **gaslighting** from Gaslight.
- **nerf him** from Nerf her.
- **mogs** from project-established contemporary slang "mog."
- **peak delulu** from peak + delulu.
- **hard reset** is not treated as glossary slang; see below.

D. NON-SLANG DIGITAL-ERA ACCOMMODATION

The following bold interventions are modern digital-era accommodations rather than strict slang:
- **hard reset**
- **admin access**

They are bolded under the Romeo Protocol's markup rule but excluded from strict slang counts.

E. EVIDENCE-GATING RULE

A lexical replacement may be:
- Strong equivalence: close semantic/pragmatic correspondence.
- Moderate equivalence: defensible metaphorical or functional correspondence.
- Loose / creative accommodation: primarily rhetorical, prosodic, digital-era, or comic transformation.

No category is represented as more exact than the evidence supports.

5. THE TEMPEST, ACT III, SCENE II
2026 LEXICAL LAYER / SHAKESPEAREAN PROSODY
======================================================================

[Enter CALIBAN, STEPHANO, and TRINCULO.]

STEPHANO

Tell me nothing. Till the barrel's drained,
let water **log off**. We stay **locked in** on wine.
Servant monster, drink to me.

TRINCULO

"Servant monster"? This island is a **shitshow**.
They say five people live upon this isle;
we are three. If the other two have our
same **skill issue**, then the state is **cooked**.

STEPHANO

Drink when I bid thee. Both thine eyes are
**lagging IRL** inside thy head.

TRINCULO

Where else should they be set? It would be **peak delulu**
to keep them in his tail.

STEPHANO

My man-monster has drowned his tongue in sack.
The sea tried hard to **cook** me; yet, **no cap**,
I swam five-and-thirty leagues before
I reached the shore. Thou shalt be lieutenant.

TRINCULO

Lieutenant, maybe. Standard? He can hardly stand.

STEPHANO

We'll not run, Monsieur Monster.

TRINCULO

Nor walk straight either. You will lie like dogs
and say as little.

STEPHANO

Mooncalf, speak once, if thou be good for speech.

CALIBAN

How does thy Honor? Let me lick thy shoe.
I'll never serve that man; his courage is **mid**.

TRINCULO

That's **cap**, you most ignorant monster.
I'm drunk enough to fight a constable.
Was ever coward filled with this much sack?

CALIBAN

See how he mocks me! Wilt thou let this
**clown behavior** slide, my lord?

STEPHANO

Trinculo, **pack it up**. One mutiny more
and thou art **cooked**. This monster is my subject;
he shall not suffer mockery from thee.

CALIBAN

I thank my noble lord. Wilt hear again
the suit I brought thee?

STEPHANO

**Bet**. Kneel down
and **lore dump** it. We two will stand and hear.

[Enter ARIEL, invisible.]

CALIBAN

As I told thee, I am bound beneath
a tyrant and a sorcerer, Prospero,
who stole this island from me by his cunning.

ARIEL, in Trinculo's **voice filter**

**Cap**.

CALIBAN

**Be so for real**, thou jesting monkey! I
would have my valiant master punish thee.
I do not lie.

STEPHANO

Trinculo, interrupt his **lore dump** once
again and I shall **hard reset** thy teeth.

TRINCULO

Why, I said nothing.

STEPHANO

Then **say less**. Proceed.

CALIBAN

By sorcery he took this isle from me.
If thy Greatness dares, make him our **main quest**:
revenge me on him. Thou hast nerve enough;
this thing beside thee has not.

STEPHANO

Certain truth.

CALIBAN

Thou shalt be lord here, and I'll serve thee.

STEPHANO

How shall we compass it? Canst bring me to him?

CALIBAN

Ay. In the afternoon he sleeps. I'll lead
thee to him there, and thou mayst kill him sleeping.

ARIEL, in Trinculo's **voice filter**

**Cap**. Thou canst not.

CALIBAN

Again? This is pure **clown behavior**.
My lord, strike him and take away his bottle.
When that's gone, he can drink the brine; I will
not show him where the fresh springs run.

STEPHANO

Trinculo, interrupt him one more word
and thou art **cooked**.

TRINCULO

What did I do? I did nothing. I'll stand farther off.

STEPHANO

Didst thou not say he lied?

ARIEL, in Trinculo's **voice filter**

**Cap**.

STEPHANO

Do I lie? Then take that.

[He beats Trinculo.]

TRINCULO

I never said it! Someone's **gaslighting** this whole scene,
then hitting me for words I never spoke.
Your bottle has you **delulu**
and deeply **out of pocket**.

CALIBAN

Ha, ha, ha!

STEPHANO

Now forward. And thou, stand farther off.

CALIBAN

As I told thee, afternoons he sleeps.
But first possess his books. They are his
**admin access** to all the spirits here.
Without those books he's **mid**, no more than I,
with not one spirit ready at command.
Burn all his books and **nerf him** down to nothing.

He keeps rich things he means to deck a house with.
But more than all, consider his fair daughter.
He calls her without equal. I have seen
no woman but my mother and this maid,
and she **mogs** Sycorax as greatest least.

STEPHANO

Is she so fine a woman?

CALIBAN

Ay, my lord.
You mean to take her for your bed and make
her bear your children.

STEPHANO

Monster, I will kill this man. His daughter
and I shall be king and queen. That's **main character syndrome**
with an actual crown. You and Trinculo
shall be my viceroys. How likes the plot?

TRINCULO

A **W**.

STEPHANO

Give me thy hand. I'm sorry that I beat thee.
But while thou livest, keep down all thy **yap**.

CALIBAN

Within this half hour Prospero will sleep.
Wilt thou destroy him then?

STEPHANO

**No cap**. I will.

ARIEL, aside

I'll carry **receipts** straight to my master.

CALIBAN

Thou makest me merry. Now let us **lock in**.
Will you sing that catch you taught me?

STEPHANO

At thy request, monster, any reason.
Come, Trinculo, let us sing.

[They sing.]

CALIBAN

**This ain't it**. That's not the tune.

[ARIEL plays the tune on tabor and pipe.]

STEPHANO

What sound is this?

TRINCULO

Our very song, but somehow gone **unsauced**;
the tune is here, the player nowhere seen.

STEPHANO

If thou art man, show thyself. If devil,
then do what thou wilt.

TRINCULO

O, forgive my sins!

STEPHANO

He that dies pays every debt. I defy thee!
Mercy upon us!

CALIBAN

Art thou afraid?

STEPHANO

No, monster. Not I.

CALIBAN

Be not afeard. The isle is full of noises,
sweet sounds and airs; no **jump scare** lives in them.
Sometimes a thousand instruments will hum
about mine ears; that whole sound **hits different**;
and sometimes voices that, if I wake
from long and heavy sleep, will make me sleep
again; and then, in dreaming, clouds methought
would open and **soft launch** riches above me,
ready still to fall, that when I waked,
I cried to dream again.

STEPHANO

This kingdom is a **W** if music's free.

CALIBAN

When Prospero is gone.

STEPHANO

That comes anon. I remember the story.

TRINCULO

The sound is moving off. Let's take this **side quest**,
then circle back and finish our **main quest**.

STEPHANO

Lead, monster. We'll follow. I would see
the player of this tune. **It slaps**. Wilt come?

TRINCULO

I'll follow, Stephano.

[They exit.]

8. ICONIC-PASSAGE PRESERVATION RULE
======================================================================

The opening of Caliban's famous speech remains substantially recognizable:

"Be not afeard. The isle is full of noises,
sweet sounds and airs..."

Reason:
The Romeo Protocol requires aggressive modernization but does not require slang to erase every durable Shakespearean line.

The adaptation therefore introduces selected contemporary collisions:
- **jump scare**
- **hits different**
- **soft launch**

while preserving the lyrical architecture and dream logic.

This follows the same editorial principle used in King Lear for:
"I am a man / more sinned against than sinning."

- orient toward five-beat Shakespearean movement;
- retain antithesis, repetition, parallelism, and dramatic pacing;
- permit inversions, contractions, substitutions, feminine endings, and deliberate slang stress;
- do not claim mechanical iambic pentameter where the line does not sustain it.

The SEA | tried HARD | to COOK | me; YET | no CAP.

By SOR | cery HE | took THIS | isle FROM | me.

Be NOT | afeard. | The ISLE | is FULL | of NOISes.

That WHOLE | sound HITS | difF'RENT | round mine EARS.

The "Be not afeard" speech is intentionally less metrically disrupted than the surrounding comedy.
