## Counting controls

1. One occurrence equals one use of a tracked slang/slang-derived expression.
2. Case-only variants are normalized for distinct-expression counting.
3. Inflectional, hyphenated, and productive variants may remain distinct in exact-form research tables unless a term-family analysis explicitly groups them.
4. Pure digital-era accommodations are tracked separately from strict slang unless reclassified with evidence.
5. Superseded drafts remain in provenance history but are excluded from canonical totals to prevent double counting.
6. Future retro-audits may change canonical counts. Any change must be recorded as a new corpus checkpoint rather than silently replacing this one.
